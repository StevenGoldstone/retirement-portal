STEVEN & BRANDÉ RETIREMENT PORTAL — VERSION 2.1

ONLY CHANGE IN THIS RELEASE

The Journey Complete widget is now calculated automatically as:

Journey Complete =
(days elapsed since July 20, 2026 ÷ total days from July 20, 2026 to October 5, 2028) × 100

The value:
- requires no manual input,
- recalculates whenever the portal opens or refreshes,
- advances automatically as each calendar day passes,
- displays 100.0% on and after October 5, 2028.

The Retirement Readiness score remains unchanged. It continues to be the average of:
- Credit-card elimination
- Escondido mortgage payoff
- Truck loan payoff

No other interface, synchronization, authentication, wording, layout, or feature was changed.

DEPLOYMENT

1. Upload all eight files inside this folder to the root of:
   https://github.com/StevenGoldstone/retirement-portal
2. Replace the existing files.
3. Commit the changes.
4. Wait 1–3 minutes.
5. Open:
   https://stevengoldstone.github.io/retirement-portal/
6. Hold Shift while clicking Reload.
7. Confirm the header says Version 2.1.
